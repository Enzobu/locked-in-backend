## Notes

- These accounts are generated by `src/DataFixtures/AppFixtures.php`.
- If fixtures are reloaded, credentials stay the same.
- Fixtures mode is controlled with `FIXTURES_MODE` (`light` or `full`).

### Fixtures Modes

- `FIXTURES_MODE=light`
  - 2 companies
  - 2 backoffice users (+ 1 super admin)
  - 2 customers
  - 2 locker bays per company
  - 3 to 4 lockers per locker bay
  - 0 to 4 reservations per locker

- `FIXTURES_MODE=full`
  - 20 companies
  - 20 backoffice users (+ 1 super admin)
  - 10 customers
  - 3 to 6 locker bays per company
  - 10 to 30 lockers per locker bay
  - 1 to 20 reservations per locker

## Loading Heavy Fixtures

This dataset is intentionally large. Load fixtures with debug disabled to avoid memory issues in Doctrine debug middlewares.

```bash
APP_ENV=dev APP_DEBUG=0 php bin/console doctrine:fixtures:load
```

or:

```bash
php bin/console doctrine:fixtures:load --no-debug
```
